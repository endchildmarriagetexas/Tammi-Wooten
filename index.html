Welcome

Welcome. My name is Tammi Wooten.

This website exists to educate, raise awareness, support survivors, and encourage meaningful conversations about child marriage and its lifelong impact.

My experience shaped my life, but it does not define my future. Today I use my voice to help others find theirs.

"A child should never have to give up a childhood to become a spouse."

— Tammi Wooten

My Story

In 2005, I was legally married in Texas at age 15. Just three months later, Texas raised the minimum marriage age to 16.

At a time when most teenagers are focused on school, friendships, and discovering who they are, I entered into a marriage that would have lasting effects on my life.

Today, I share my experience to shine a light on the realities of child marriage and advocate for stronger protections for children.

Welcome

My name is Tammi Wooten. I am a Texas child marriage survivor, advocate, and public speaker.

I share my story to help educate communities, support survivors, and encourage meaningful conversations about the lifelong impact child marriage can have on children and families.

"Survival was my beginning. Advocacy became my purpose."

My Story

At age 15, I entered a legal marriage in Texas. Like many survivors, that experience changed the course of my life.

Only three months later, Texas raised the minimum marriage age from 15 to 16.

Today, I speak openly about my experience because awareness matters, and every survivor deserves to know they are not alone.

My Story

My name is Tammi Wooten.


I was legally married in Texas at the age of 15. At a time when most teenagers are focused on school, friendships, and planning their futures, I entered a marriage that would shape the course of my life.


Only three months after my marriage, Texas changed the law and raised the minimum age for marriage to 16. That reality has remained with me throughout my life.


Today, I share my story to bring awareness to child marriage, encourage honest conversations, support survivors, and help people understand the lifelong consequences these decisions can have on young people.

"Survival was my beginning. Advocacy became my purpose."

My Advocacy Work

Child Marriage Awareness

Sharing my story and educating others about the realities and long-term impact of child marriage.

Supporting Survivors

Helping survivors know they are not alone and encouraging them to find their own voice.

Public Education

Providing information, resources, and discussions that help communities understand these important issues.

Policy Awareness

Encouraging conversations about laws and protections that impact children and vulnerable individuals.

My Mission

Every child deserves a childhood.

My mission is to use my experience to educate, inspire, and create awareness about the effects of child marriage while supporting efforts that protect children and empower survivors.
